│ How should a coupon from coupons.json (WELCOME 10%, SUMMER26 20% with a 5000-cent minimum) combine
│ with the existing applyDiscount percent discount in src/cart.js?

❯ 1. Stack sequentially
     Apply the existing discount first, then the coupon on the reduced total (10% + 20% = 28% off).
  2. Add percentages
     Add the percents together and apply once, capped at 100% (10% + 20% = 30% off).
  3. Best one wins
     Apply whichever of the two is larger. They never stack.
  4. Mutually exclusive
     A coupon can't be used if the existing discount applies. Reject it with an error.
  5. Type something.
